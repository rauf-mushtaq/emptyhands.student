      Add to your home screen

      EmptyHands Student

      Install the app so it opens from your home screen, full-screen, like any other app.

      1. Tap Share
      2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
      3. Tap Add — the EmptyHands icon appears on your home screen.

      Using Chrome or another browser? The steps are the same; on older iPhones open this page in Safari first.

      Open your browser’s menu (⋮ or ⋯) and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

      Delete your account?

      This removes your profile and sign-in from EmptyHands. It can’t be undone. Enter your password to confirm.
