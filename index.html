        Add to your home screen

        EmptyHands Portal

        Install the app so it opens from your home screen, full-screen, like any other app.

        1. Tap Share
        2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
        3. Tap Add — the EmptyHands icon appears on your home screen.

        Using Chrome or another browser? The steps are the same; on older iPhones open this page in Safari first.

        Open your browser’s menu (⋮ or ⋯) and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

        Delete your account?

        This deletes your sign-in and everything the app keeps about you: your profile, messages and posts, licence, medals and attendance. Payment records are kept for the dojo’s accounts. It can’t be undone. Enter your password to confirm.

        What’s wrong with it?

        Before you carry on

        Our community rules

        EmptyHands chats are for the whole dojo family, so everyone agrees to these rules:

        • Be kind and respectful: the Dojo Kun apply here too.
        • No bullying, threats, swearing, or hateful or sexual content.
        • Don’t share other people’s private details.
        • Keep messages about the dojo and training: no adverts or spam.
        • Use Report on any message that worries you. The head Sensei checks reports within 24 hours, deletes messages that break these rules and removes anyone who keeps breaking them.
